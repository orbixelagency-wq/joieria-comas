# PRODUCT.md — Joieria Comas

## What it is
Marketing website (single-page scroll narrative) for **Joieria Comas**, a family house of
jewellers and watchmakers in Santa Coloma de Farners (Girona, Catalonia), trading since
**1862** (guild roots cited as early as 1841). Run by the Comas family — Xavier Comas and
Josep Comas — across two adjacent shops on Carrer Pare Lluís Rodés, plus workshops in
Vidreres and Salt.

## Mode
Persuade. A first-time visitor should understand within seconds: a 160-year family house
with its **own jewellery + watchmaking workshop**, official **Pandora** and **Daniel
Wellington** stockist, that repairs, creates, transforms and engraves in-house.

## Audience & scene
Local and comarca (la Selva) customers looking for engagement/wedding rings, gold & silver
jewellery, brand watches, watch repair, and heirloom transformation. Also destination
shoppers researching the shops before visiting. Viewed mostly on phones, some desktop.

## The differentiator (the thing to prove)
**Taller propi** — own workshop for both jewellery and technical watchmaking, no
intermediaries. Six generations of accumulated craft.

## Services (workshop)
- Technical watch repair: antique wind-up & wall clocks → modern automatic & quartz;
  crystal replacement, battery change, water-resistance/hermeticity, complex mechanical calibres.
- Jewellery creation from scratch + transformation of inherited/antique pieces.
- Ultrasonic cleaning & polishing of gold and silver.
- Personalised engraving (names, dates, initials) on rings, wedding bands, watch backs.

## Catalogue / brands
- 18k gold (750) & first-law (sterling) silver; engagement rings & wedding bands.
- **Pandora** — official authorised stockist (charms, bracelets).
- **Daniel Wellington** — official collections.
